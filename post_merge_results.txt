3. ANALYSIS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A. Duplicate UPD bug caused ~10-21% throughput undercount in no-PP runs
   The NO_PP_4STAGE_SCHEDULE had an explicit UPD task that was then followed by
   a second UPD synthesized by schedule_to_dag, causing every no-PP iteration to
   run the optimizer twice. PP=2 runs were unaffected (INTERLEAVED_1F1B had no
   explicit UPD). After the fix, no-PP throughput increases 9-21%:

   Config       Old (incorrect)  New (corrected)  Δ
   ──────────   ───────────────  ───────────────  ──────
   baseline           13 743          16 177       +17.7%
   no-pp dp-nozero    26 034          31 281       +20.2%
   no-pp zero-1       28 445          31 770       +11.7%
   no-pp zero-2       26 608          32 163       +20.9%
   no-pp zero-3       29 017          31 547        +8.7%

   Variance in speedup is consistent with the optimizer step being ~23ms for ZeRO
   stages (reduce-scatter + optim + all-gather) vs ~9.5ms for plain no-DP optimizer.
   The extra UPD consumed a larger fraction of total time for dp-nozero and zero-2
   (which had pathological 12905/13703 imbalance in the old run).

B. PP=2 per-GPU efficiency is now correctly below baseline (-13 to -15%)
   With the old duplicate-UPD baseline, PP=2 appeared to match or beat single-GPU
   (0.2-2.5% above). This was an artifact: the baseline was artificially slowed by
   the double optimizer step. With the corrected baseline (16,177 tok/s/GPU), PP=2
   shows the expected pipeline bubble overhead: -13% to -15% per GPU for
   non-bucketed, -21% to -25% for bucketed.

C. No-PP ZeRO stages are now very similar (within ~3% of dp-nozero)
   All no-PP ZeRO variants land at 31.3–32.2k global tok/s, compared to 31.3k for
   dp-nozero. The ZeRO collective overhead (RS+AG ≈ same volume as all-reduce) is
   nearly fully overlapped with computation at DP=2. Previous results showed larger
   spread (26.6–29.0k) due to double-UPD contamination magnifying small timing noise
   and the suspicious zero-2 imbalance.

D. PP=2 non-bucketed: all configs still ~28k tok/s per DP-rank (unchanged)
   ZeRO overheads are fully overlapped with computation at PP=2. The ZeRO stages
   are within 2% of each other and of dp-nozero. This finding is unchanged.

E. Bucketing hurts throughput at PP=2 (~10–14% regression) — unchanged
   Non-bucketed PP=2 dp-nozero reaches 55,073 global tok/s vs 48,927 bucketed (−11%).
   Consistent across all ZeRO levels. DAG overhead from fine-grained tasks and
   per-bucket NCCL calls that don't individually saturate NIC. For this config,
   bucketing is a net negative without a benefit from model-parallel comm overlap.
